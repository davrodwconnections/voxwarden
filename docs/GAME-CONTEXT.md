# Character context

Voxwarden can use the current character's level, XP, money, professions, equipment/durability, talents, position, quest log and available nearby world facts. Expanded observations add spell samples, health/power/stats/pet, carried items, personal bank, learned recipes/materials, merchant stock/prices and available trainer services. Other players' identities and guild/account/other-character banks are excluded.

To refresh observations, open your own profession, personal bank, vendor or trainer window. For crafting advice, open the relevant profession and leave its recipe list visible. The addon never changes filters, expands categories, crafts, buys, sells, trains or starts auction searches. Auctionator's installed stable v1 API supplies observed quotes when available; without it or without relevant prices, profitability remains unknown. Update quotes using your normal auction workflow.

Crafting estimates value gathered materials at their market opportunity cost. They are per craft, before auction fees/deposits, and do not establish demand, tradeability or guaranteed profit. Unknown ingredients/output or old/missing prices prevent a numeric margin. Whole-day quote ages are coarser than live prices. Vendor sell values describe what the vendor pays you, not material purchase cost.

Lists are bounded samples: up to8 recipes with8 required reagents each,48 active spells,32 distinct carried items,32 personal bank items and12 merchant/trainer rows. Recipe filters and hidden categories can limit coverage; absence from a sample does not prove absence from the character. The most recently opened profession is the recipe context. Captures keep their original date after windows close; combat restricts reads and transmission. Frame limits can omit entire sections, identified in the request. Voice asks can prioritize relevant sections without removing quest identities.

New reads require a bridge advertising `xctx`, companion on and game context on. Existing privacy switches still apply to both written and spoken questions. A source edit or passing simulated tests does not establish compatibility with a live client: restart WoW after adding GameContext.lua and verify fresh recipe/bank observations in the running app.

API references: [Forever trade skills](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/TradeSkillUIDocumentation.lua), [recipe types](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/TradeSkillUITypesDocumentation.lua), [spellbook](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/SpellBookDocumentation.lua), and Auctionator's installed Source/API/v1/GetAuctionPrice.lua/GetAuctionAge.lua. Functions are probed and guarded; unavailable variants remain unknown.
